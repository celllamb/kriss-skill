## Variant constraints
- H01 uses the C11 radial-hub geometry as an optional topology; decorative node accent dots are not used.
- H02–H06 use the KRISS standard template chrome. The original background JPEG is reused byte-for-byte. Its title artwork and footer are not reconstructed with CSS dots/bands. Standard title geometry and the content safe area follow the canonical reference. See `original-artwork-contract.md`.
- Variant-specific geometry is restricted to the `.content` region.
